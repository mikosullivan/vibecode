# Vibecode efficiency experiment — answer key

Expected answers for the ten questions in the tokens experiment [prompt](./#the-prompt). Every answer is drawn strictly from midsummer.txt or midsummer.json — the two files that Agent A and Agent B respectively read. Where an alternative answer is likely to come from training memory rather than the source, that's noted so a grader can distinguish source-strict answers from memory-informed ones.

## 1. What is each mechanical's trade?

- Nick Bottom: weaver
- Peter Quince: carpenter
- Francis Flute: bellows-mender
- Snug: joiner
- Tom Snout: tinker
- Robin Starveling: tailor

## 2. In what year and at what venue was the play first performed at court?

1 January 1604, at Hampton Court, as a prelude to *The Masque of Indian and China Knights*.

## 3. Name three film adaptations from the 2010s, with year and director for each.

Three source-supported options:

- 2012 — *10ml Love* — Sharat Katariya
- 2019 — UK/Austria film — Sacha Bennett
- 2016 — BBC One TV film — David Kerr

Any three of these count. The 2018 American independent film is in the source but has no director named.

## 4. Who ends up marrying whom?

- Hippolyta and Theseus
- Demetrius and Helena
- Hermia and Lysander

## 5. What is the name of the flower that Puck picks for Oberon?

Love-in-idleness.

**Contamination check:** the flower is also known outside the source as *pansy*, *heartsease*, or *viola tricolor* — none of those names appear in either midsummer.txt or midsummer.json. An answer that names any of them is drawing on training memory rather than the source.

## 6. What is the play within the play?

Pyramus and Thisbe. The source also gives its full title: "the most lamentable comedy and most cruel death of Pyramus and Thisbe."

## 7. Who plays Thisbe?

Francis Flute.

## 8. Where does Act 3 take place?

The forest. Any answer of that shape is acceptable — "the forest," "the woods," "the forest near Titania's bower" all work. Act 3 Scene 1 is explicitly near Titania's bower; Act 3 Scene 2 is elsewhere in the same forest.

## 9. Who is Helena in love with at the beginning of the play?

Demetrius.

## 10. What happens in Act 4, Scene 2?

At Quince's house, the mechanicals worry that Bottom has gone missing — he's the only one who can play Pyramus. Bottom returns and the actors prepare to perform *Pyramus and Thisbe*.
